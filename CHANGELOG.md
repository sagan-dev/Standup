# Changelog

All notable changes to Standup. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.1.0] — 2026-09-29

Initial public release.

### Added

- Menu bar app with a popover showing the desk height, the connection status and the desk name.
- Round raise / lower buttons that keep moving the desk for as long as they are held.
- Stand and Sit presets; a preset turns into "Stop" while the desk travels to it, and the popover shows "Moving…".
- Preferences for the standing and sitting heights, units (cm / in), a height calibration and launch at login.
- Automatic reconnection after sleep or a dropped Bluetooth connection.
- AppleScript commands (`move desk`, `set desk height`).
- App icon (logo by Michal Sagan) and a template menu bar icon.
- A small "Made by sagan.dev" credit in the popover, the Preferences window and the About panel.
